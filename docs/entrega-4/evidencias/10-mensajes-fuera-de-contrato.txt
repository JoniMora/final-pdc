# Comandos ejecutados desde la carpeta reserva-cupos.

## Un comando reservar sin reserva_id ni actividad_id

$ curl -s -u demo:demo_local_2026 -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -X POST http://localhost:15672/api/exchanges/%2F/amq.default/publish -d '{"routing_key":"reservas.comandos.0","payload":"{\"comando\":\"reservar\",\"tenant_id\":\"01a1542d-7ee2-7d8b-b3b3-40474b8dec7e\"}","payload_encoding":"string","properties":{"message_id":"manual-1"}}'; echo; sleep 2
{"routed":true}
